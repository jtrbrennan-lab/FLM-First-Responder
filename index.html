Medic 1 Flashcards

A 7-day study plan for the FLM TCCC / Combat Lifesaver Supplement (v2.0, August 2023). 195 cards, each citing its page.

Must-know cards cover the 9 skills-test stations and the numbers you'll be asked for.

How to use it

  • Set your class start date. The plan counts back 7 days and highlights each day's set.
  • Read the whole supplement once on Day 1, then work the cards. Answer from memory before you tap, and mark yourself honestly.
  • A missed card comes back a few cards later, and stays in your missed pile until you get it right on the first try in a later round.
  • Practice the skills with your hands, not just in your head, and sleep well the night before class.
  • Your class date and progress are saved in this browser only, so each trainee keeps their own.
